# Cover style guide

## Visual character

Aim for a bright, energetic creator-thumbnail aesthetic: clean white space, oversized editorial type, sticker-like depth, pastel technology accents, and a friendly real-person cutout. The result should feel credible and handcrafted rather than like a generic AI poster.

## Hierarchy

1. Main headline: instantly readable and visually dominant.
2. Portrait: recognizable face, expressive gesture, clean silhouette.
3. Supporting object: phone, app screen, product UI, document, or subject-specific prop.
4. Brand and decoration: small, optional, and never competing with the title.

Keep the key message understandable in under one second at roughly 25% display size.

## Typography

- Prefer a very heavy Chinese sans-serif/Hei display face.
- Use black fill with a thick white outline.
- Add two restrained offset shadows: lavender/blue and mint.
- Break Chinese headlines by meaning, not at arbitrary character counts.
- Use at most three headline rows.
- Keep subtitles shorter and clearly secondary.
- Avoid thin type, gradients inside glyphs, excessive rotation, and more than two font families.

## Color

Suggested palette:

- background: `#FFFFFF` or `#FAFBFF`
- text: `#050505`
- mint: `#52E1BC`
- lavender: `#9AA4FF`
- pale blue: `#DCEEFF`
- success green: `#16CC82`

Use warm accents only for small highlights. Maintain strong contrast.

## Portrait treatment

- Cut out cleanly with believable hair edges.
- Add a narrow white border and a subtle lavender shadow when separation is needed.
- Brighten and even the complexion, reduce oily shine, and apply natural skin smoothing while preserving pores, contours, and recognizable features.
- Prefer an upper-body crop with hands visible when the gesture contributes to the story.
- Freely adapt the pose and expression to the topic while preserving identity.
- Add a handheld microphone for creator-led commentary, tutorials, or announcements when it strengthens the story.
- Allow pointing gestures, phones, and other topic-relevant props; keep hands and object contact anatomically plausible.

## Supporting graphics

One large story-specific supporting object is stronger than many small objects. Render devices with plausible perspective, consistent lighting, and clean edges. Treat UI screenshots as supplied assets: do not rewrite or hallucinate their content.

Use small cards, dotted paths, arrows, sparkles, and soft brush strokes only to guide the eye or fill awkward negative space.

## Reference images

The bundled 3:4 and 4:3 images demonstrate hierarchy, portrait placement, outlined type, phone mockup scale, pastel brush texture, and decorative density. Analyze them as style references only. Do not reproduce their person, WorkBuddy branding, wording, or app interface in unrelated covers.
